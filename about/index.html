About

Juan De Marco makes images, objects and software. The work moves between drawing, color studies, digital tools and built form, guided by an interest in light, atmosphere and how space is perceived.

He trained as an architect, with a Master of Architecture from Princeton University and a Bachelor of Architecture from the Illinois Institute of Technology, and has worked at Snøhetta, Reiser + Umemoto and FXCollaborative. He is a licensed architect.
