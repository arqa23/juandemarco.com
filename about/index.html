About

Juan De Marco is a licensed architect and artist who makes architecture, objects and drawings.

His work is guided by history, by cultures, and by the people who inhabit the world we live in, with a deep appreciation and curiosity about the ways artists and designers have engaged with the built environment in the past.

He holds a Master of Architecture from Princeton University and a Bachelor of Architecture from the Illinois Institute of Technology, and has worked at the offices of Snøhetta, Reiser + Umemoto and FXCollaborative on a range of cultural and institutional projects.
